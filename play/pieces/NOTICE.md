To swap in a different set, drop 12 files named `wK wQ wR wB wN wP bK bQ bR bB
bN bP` (`.svg`) into this directory. Lichess ships several under permissive
terms — `rhosgfx` is CC0, `chessnut` is Apache 2.0.
